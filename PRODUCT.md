# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Public users tracking personal finances. They sign up (or start anonymously) to plan monthly income and expenses, checking in a few times a week to confirm expected amounts against what actually happened. Shared cashflows mean a second audience: people invited to view a plan who may not be finance-minded. The job to be done: know whether the months ahead hold up, and reconcile the plan with reality in seconds.

## Product Purpose

Cashflow Tracker is a forward-looking personal finance planner built around expected vs actual amounts. Users lay out income (salary, freelance, rental) and expenses (groceries, utilities, subscriptions) across a board of months, quick-confirm entries as they land, drag items between months when timing shifts, and watch the cumulative balance curve respond. Success: a user trusts the projected balance enough to make real decisions with it, and reconciling a month takes under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Quiet confidence in the register of Linear and the Stripe Dashboard: numbers first, zero drama, craft expressed through tight typography and restrained color rather than decoration. The tone of a reliable instrument, not a coach or a mascot.

## Anti-references

- Generic fintech SaaS: navy/gold gradients, hero-metric cards, glassmorphism, dashboard-template clichés.
- Toy budgeting apps: mascots, confetti, gamified streaks, oversized friendly illustration.
- Corporate banking portals: gray bureaucratic forms, dated tables, compliance-heavy density.

## Design Principles

1. **The delta is the product.** Expected vs actual is the core insight; every surface should make that gap legible at a glance before anything else.
2. **Precision earns trust.** Aligned figures, consistent number formatting, and restrained color do the persuading; decoration would undermine the numbers.
3. **Fast to confirm, forgiving to change.** Quick-confirm and drag-between-months are the core loops; keep them one gesture, make them reversible, never punish a timing change.
4. **Readable by an invitee.** A shared cashflow must parse in seconds for someone who has never seen the app; income vs expense and the running balance need no legend-hunting.
5. **Anonymous first touch sells the tool.** The pre-signup experience is the pitch; empty states and the anonymous board must demonstrate value, not gate it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target. Apply sensible defaults: readable contrast on numeric text, keyboard-reachable alternatives for drag-and-drop moves, and respect for reduced-motion preferences.
